# Avatar builder: the concept

After the Vibe Card reveal in onboarding, a "Make your avatar" step offers six short choices on one screen. Each
choice is a row of round tiles, and the cartoon at the top changes as you tap. Nothing is guessed from a photo or a
name, and Skip is always there.

1. Skin tone: six tones, from light to deep.
2. Face: smile, grin or calm.
3. Hair: short, bob, long, curly, bun or bald, plus a hair colour.
4. Head covering: none, hijab, turban or cap, each with a colour.
5. Glasses, and a beard or moustache.
6. Top colour, from the app's pastels.

Your flatmate type is added on its own: the rhythm sets the background (sunrise, moon or starry sky) and the habit is
the thing you hold. Change your answers and the avatar follows.

Where it appears:
- as the 40 px picture on home cards ("Priya lives here");
- on Tribe and Escape cards;
- in chat;
- in Account, where you can edit it any time;
- large and animated on the Vibe Card, waving when it opens.

How it's built:
- It's drawn from our own SVG parts and bundled in the app, with no outside avatar service.
- It's saved as a few small choices on the profile, not as an image.
- With reduced motion turned on, it stays still.
