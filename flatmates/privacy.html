1. Who we are

FindMeFlatMate (the website findmeflatmate.com and the FindMeFlatMate Android app) helps people find a room, and the right people to share it with. It is run by Richa Wadhwani ("we", "us"). We decide why and how your personal data is used, which makes us the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

This policy covers the website and the app. When you sign in, we ask you to read it. By using FindMeFlatMate you agree to your data being used as described here.

2. What we collect

When you sign in

  • Your mobile number, to sign you in with a one-time code sent by SMS, and your first name.

Your profile

  • Whether you're looking for a room or for a flatmate.
  • What you do (working, student and so on, and a few words about it) and your generation (Gen Z, millennial, Gen X, or none).
  • Your answers to 12 living-style questions (bedtimes, cooking, food, smoking, drinks, guests, noise, chores, pets and similar), and the music and hobbies you pick.
  • If you're looking for a room: the city, areas and rent budget you choose, and whether owners and possible flatmates may find you.

Rooms you list

  • The room's details, rent, deposit, extra expenses, photos and description.
  • Details about your household that you choose to share: how many people live there, what they do, their age range and how the home lives.
  • The contact name and number for the listing, the address and an optional map pin.

Using FindMeFlatMate

  • Requests, invites and the notes you send with them, visit times, saved homes, tribe matches, and broker reports.
  • Flatsplits: your groups, the names and numbers of the people you add to them, bills, payments, and your UPI ID if you add it. Only add people who are happy to be added.
  • FlatMart: the items you sell (photos, price, description) and the offers you make or get.
  • Payments: which pass you bought, the amount with GST, and Razorpay's order and payment references. Your card, UPI or bank details go straight to Razorpay. We never see or store them.
  • Notifications: a code from your phone or browser that lets us send you notifications.

Where you came from

  • When you first visit, we note the link that brought you: its campaign tags (utm_source, utm_campaign and similar), Google or Meta click codes (gclid, fbclid), the website you came from and the page you landed on. If you then create an account, this is saved with it. If a friend invited you, we note their invite code.

On your device

  • The app keeps your settings, drafts, and a copy of the app for slow connections in your browser's or phone's storage. This stays on your device.
  • The 1-minute Vibe quiz on our website doesn't need an account: your answers stay on your device. If you share your Vibe Card link, the link carries your answers and the first name you typed, so anyone you send it to can see them.

We don't ask for your email, date of birth, ID documents or contacts list, and we don't track your location in the background. The map only uses your location when you tap "I'm there now" while placing a pin, and only after your phone asks your permission.

3. How we use it

  • To run FindMeFlatMate: sign you in, work out your vibe match with each home or person, show listings, deliver requests, invites and messages, and send notifications.
  • To share contact details, but only when both sides have said yes (see who sees what).
  • To keep brokers and misuse out: for example, one person can list at most two addresses, and reported listings are taken down.
  • To take payments for passes, keep invoices, and meet GST and other legal requirements.
  • To give referral rewards, and to work out which of our ads and links bring people who list or find a room, so we spend less on ads that don't work.
  • To show public totals, such as how many people are looking for a room in an area. These are counts, and nobody is named in them.

We don't sell your personal data, rent it out, or let other companies use it for their own ads.

5. Services we use

These companies run parts of FindMeFlatMate for us and only get what their part needs:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and photo storage. Our database is hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • An SMS provider, through Supabase: sends your sign-in code to your number.
  • Razorpay: takes payments for passes.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers notifications to the Android app.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts our website.
  • OpenStreetMap and Photon (by komoot): show maps and search for areas. When you use the map or the area search, they receive the map area or search text and your IP address.
  • Google Fonts: serves the typeface the app uses, which means Google sees your IP address when it loads.
  • Meta and Google ads measurement, on the website only (see cookies and ads).

Some of these companies may process data outside India, under their own security and privacy terms. We may also share data if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or to deal with fraud or abuse.

6. Cookies and ads measurement

The app itself doesn't use cookies for tracking. On the website (not the Android app), we may use the Meta Pixel and Google tags (Google Analytics and Google Ads) to measure our own ads. They tell Meta and Google when someone:

  • opens our page for people with a room, or taps "List my room";
  • creates an account (as an owner or a seeker), lists a room or sends a request; or
  • buys a pass, with the plan and price.

We add the city, area or role involved, but never your name, number, messages or anything you type. Meta and Google also collect information themselves, such as your IP address, browser and their own cookies (for example _fbp, _ga and _gcl_au), and use it under their own policies: the Meta Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

You can block these cookies in your browser's settings, and choose which ads you see in Meta's ad preferences and Google's My Ad Center. FindMeFlatMate works the same either way.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, listings and activity: as long as you have an account. Account → Delete my account deletes it straight away: your profile and answers, listings and their photos, requests, invites, visits, saved homes, tribe matches, FlatMart items and offers, passes and notification codes. If you write to us instead, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Flatsplits groups you share with other people stay for them after you delete your account, as their record of who paid what, with the name and number they added you under. Your UPI ID is removed from them.
  • Payment records and invoices: as long as Indian tax law requires, even after you delete your account, without your name or number.
  • Where you came from: as long as your account exists.
  • Database backups are kept for a short time and then overwritten.
  • What's stored on your device stays there until you sign out, clear the app's data or uninstall it.

8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, and who we've shared it with;
  • Correct or update it. Most of it you can change yourself in the app: your answers under Account → How you live, your listings under My flat;
  • Delete your account and data, yourself under Account → Delete my account, or withdraw your consent (withdrawing means we close your account, as FindMeFlatMate can't work without this data);
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or can't act yourself; and
  • Complain to our grievance officer (below), and then to the Data Protection Board of India if you're not happy with our answer.

To do any of this, write to privacy@findmeflatmate.com and tell us the mobile number on your account. We may check that the request really comes from you before we act on it.

10. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database lets people reach only what this policy says they can: a flat's address and contact number are stored separately from the public listing and are only given out once both sides say yes. Payments are handled by Razorpay. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us at once at privacy@findmeflatmate.com if you think something is wrong. If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

11. Age limit

FindMeFlatMate is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child has signed up, tell us and we'll delete the account.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change matters (for example, a new use of your data), we'll tell you in the app before it applies.
