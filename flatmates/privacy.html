5. Services we use (Data Processors)

These companies run parts of FindMeFlatMate for us and get only what their part needs:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and photo storage, hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Meta (WhatsApp): sends your sign-in code on WhatsApp. It receives your number and the code.
  • MSG91: sends your sign-in code by SMS when you choose SMS. It receives your number and the code.
  • Razorpay: takes payments. It receives the amount, the plan, your account ID, and your name and number to fill in its payment form.
  • Google Maps and Places: maps and area search. Google receives the map area or what you type, and your IP address.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers notifications to the Android app. Google receives your device's notification code and the notification text (which can include a first name and a listing's title).
  • Google Fonts: the typeface. Google sees your IP address when it loads.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts our website. It sees your IP address when you open it.

Some of these process data outside India, under their own security terms. On our website we may later use the Meta Pixel and Google tags to measure our own ads; they are switched off today, and we'll update this policy before switching them on. We may also share data if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or to deal with fraud.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything tied to it (profile, listings and photos, requests, visits, saves, tribe matches, FlatMart, passes, notification codes, party settings and requests, help requests): until you delete your account. Deleting it removes them straight away.
  • What stays after you delete your account:
    • Flatsplits groups you share with others stay for them, as their record of who paid what, under the name and number they added you with. Your UPI ID is removed.
    • Payment records stay as long as tax law requires, no longer linked to your account. Razorpay keeps its own records.
    • Your name on a party's guest list (if you said you're coming through an invite link) stays with that party.
    • Sign-in and abuse records with your number or IP address: kept two days, then removed by a clean-up that runs as people use the app (so occasionally a little longer).
  • Blocks: until you unblock, or either account is deleted. Reports: two years, then deleted; if either account is deleted sooner, the report stays without the link to it, so earlier reports can still be checked.

  • On your device: until you clear the app's data, uninstall it, or delete your account (signing out keeps it). The Android app's data may be included in your phone's own backup if you have that switched on.

8. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent as easily as you gave it:

  • All of it: delete your account (Account → Delete my account). FindMeFlatMate can't work without this data, so withdrawing means closing the account.
  • Parts of it, in Account: stop owners or possible flatmates finding you, switch off parties nearby, or turn off notifications in your phone's or browser's settings.

Withdrawing doesn't undo what was lawfully done before.
