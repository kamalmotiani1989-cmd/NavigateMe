## Post A · for people looking (checklist)
Moving to Pune and looking for a room? 7 things to check before you pay a deposit:
1. Who actually lives there, and how (sleep times, food, guests, smoking).
2. The real monthly cost: rent + maid + Wi-Fi + electricity split.
3. Whether the person showing it is the owner or a broker (brokers take a month's rent).
4. Deposit on paper, with the date it comes back.
5. Notice period, and what happens if a flatmate leaves early.
6. Water in summer, parking, and the society's rules on bachelors.
7. Your number: don't post it publicly; share it once you have said yes to meeting.
We are building FindMeFlatMate for exactly this: every room shows how the people there live, with a vibe score against you, and your number is shared only when the owner says yes. No brokers, ever. Launching soon: <link>
