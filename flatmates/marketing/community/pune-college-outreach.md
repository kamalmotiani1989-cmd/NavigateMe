## 6. Value post for a student channel (only where the admin has said yes; one per channel per week, wording varied)

Getting a room near <college> next semester? Before you pay anything:
1. See it in person. Fake PG offers to parents of new students are a known Pune scam; nothing is paid before you stand
   in the room.
2. Ask who lives there and how: sleep times, food, guests, smoking, chores. It decides more than the rent.
3. Owner or broker? A broker's fee is a month's rent for a phone number.
4. Deposit on paper, with the date it comes back, and the notice period.
5. Tenant verification: Pune Police made online verification mandatory for students in PGs and hostels this October.
   Do it; it protects you.
6. Don't post your number publicly. Share it once you've said yes to meeting.
We are building FindMeFlatMate for this: rooms show how the people there live, with a vibe score against you, and your
number is shared only when the owner says yes. No brokers. Launching soon in Pune:
