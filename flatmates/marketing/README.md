# Marketing

What FindMeFlatMate says about itself, and the packs made so far. The product facts come from `../README.md`; never
claim a feature or a number that is not there or in a cited source.

**One line.** Find a flatmate who lives like you. No brokers, no guesswork.
**Tagline.** Find your vibe. Find your flatmate.

**Voice.** Plain, warm, a bit dry. Short sentences. Indian English, city names spelt Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Mumbai,
Delhi NCR. Gen Z-friendly without slang that dates in a month. No exclamation marks in a row, no "revolutionary", no
fake urgency. Numbers only when they are real (the app's own prices, or a cited report).

**Who we talk to.**
- Seekers, 21 to 28, moving within or into a metro, tired of brokers and WhatsApp-group randoms.
- Listers: a flatmate whose flatmate is leaving (picks the next one), the person leaving who needs a replacement
  (deposit on the line), and owners renting a room, a bed or a whole flat (zero brokerage, tenants who fit).

**What only we have.** Vibe score on every request (both sides answer the same living-style questions) · owners choose,
numbers swap only on a yes · Tribe (find the people first, then the flat) · Escape (owners invite seekers who fit, 3-day
answer deadline) · the app stays after the move: Flatsplits (rent and bills by UPI, pays-on-time badge), FlatMart
(moving-out sale page), parties · no brokers, ever · 5 free contacts, then passes from ₹500.

**Prices (plus GST).** Room Pass ₹500 (30 days) · Tribe Pass ₹500 (7 days) · Premium ₹1,500 · Escape Pass ₹500 (7 days)
· Featured listing ₹199 (built, off until launch) · FlatMart Boost ₹49, pack of 5 ₹199, Moving-out sale ₹149 · referrals:
2 friends = 1 free contact.

**Status.** The site shows Coming soon until launch; the Android app is in private preview. Say "launching soon", never
"download now", until the hold is lifted.

**Packs in this folder.**
- `genz-ads-pack.md`: three 20-second seeker ads (scripts, on-screen text, AI-video prompts).
- `lister-campaign-pack.md`: Instagram, Meta ads and Google Ads for the three lister personas (copy, keywords,
  targeting, budgets, UTM scheme, landing page).
- `lister-reels-captions.md`: ten lister reels (hooks, captions, hashtags, voice scripts).
The finished videos, creatives and the logo files are with Kamal; the packs describe them.
