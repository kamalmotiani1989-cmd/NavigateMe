# FindMeFlatMate · Feature creatives in English rhyme (30)

Written by Mark, 13 November 2026, on Kamal's request: English rhymes, not Hinglish, two creatives for every feature in the app.
Same style and brand as the Pune set (`pune-creatives.md`): Urbanist, #1C1917 with the rhyming line in #C2410C, #57534E for the
small line, the logo files, a real demo-mode app screen in a phone, a pastel panel for a photo, and "Launching soon ·
findmeflatmate.com". The couplets are set in bold sentence case, not capitals, so two lines read easily on a phone.

**Files** (repo root, not published): `design/feature-rhymes/feed/<id>-feed.png` (1080×1350), `story/<id>-story.png`
(1080×1920), `contact-sheet.png`. The photo area is the same as in `design/pune-creatives/photo-guides/`.

**Rules kept.** Every rhyme carries one benefit, and the small line under it states the fact from `flatmates/README.md`.
No counts of homes or people, no alcohol, no "only" claims about matching, parties at 21+, posting as "area and rent to start".

**AI photo prompts.** Paste this style block before each prompt: Photoreal, vertical 4:5, natural light, a modest
middle-class Pune flat or street. Indian people in their early to mid 20s, casual clothes. No logos, no brand names, no
text, no signs, no alcohol, no cigarettes. Keep the right third of the frame calm (the phone sits there).

**Links.** `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=feature_rhymes&utm_content=<id>`; owner-side creatives
also carry `host=Pune` so they land on the owner page.
