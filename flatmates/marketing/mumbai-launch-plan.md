## 4 · What changes versus Pune, and why

| What | Pune plan | Mumbai plan | Why, with source or marked estimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Click and impression cost | baseline | planned at 1.5× Pune | No Mumbai-vs-Pune Meta benchmark exists in public. Agency guides put Mumbai in the Tier 1 CPM group and leave Pune out (https://sotrosinfotech.com/blog/paid-social-ads-benchmarks-india-southeast-asia-2026/, seen 8 Nov 2026); one agency blog claims Google real-estate keywords cost up to 3× more in Mumbai than Pune (https://www.mayankdigitallabs.in/blog/facebook-vs-google-ads-india-roi-2026, unverified). **1.5× is my estimate**, chosen between "same" and the blog's 3×; week one replaces it with the account's own numbers. |
| Cost per listing I hold to | under ₹200 | under ₹300 | Follows from the 1.5× estimate. |
| Daily budget, full | ₹2,500 | ₹3,500 | 1.4× money so each ad set still clears Meta's learning phase in a dearer market. |
| Areas | 11 | 10 of the app's 13 | Mumbai is three separate corridors (western line, central and harbour, Thane and Navi Mumbai); fewer pins per corridor keeps the data readable. |
| Owner's brokerage saving | "a month's rent" | the same line, bigger in rupees | Mumbai custom is one month's rent as brokerage (https://housing.com/news/what-are-the-brokerage-charges-for-renting-a-flat-in-india, seen 8 Nov 2026). A CREDAI-MCHI figure puts Mumbai's average 1BHK rent at ₹43,138 a month, roughly double Bengaluru and Delhi NCR (https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/business/rental-rates-rise-in-mumbai-compared-to-other-metros-huge-disparity-between-average-salaries-and-rental-costs-in-city-credai-mchi, report date not shown in the result); Housing.com's Pune guide gives ₹10,000 to 15,000 for a 1BHK (https://housing.com/news/cost-of-living-in-pune, undated). So P3 (owners) should convert better in Mumbai; I move P3 to 35% and P1 to 35% in Mumbai from week two if week one agrees. |
| Deposit stakes (P2) | "deposit on the line" | same hook, stronger | Mumbai deposits run 2 to 3 months' rent in the suburbs and 4 to 6 in premium areas per a rental guide (https://houssed.com/blog/guides/best-areas-for-rent-in-bkc, seen 8 Nov 2026); the replacement persona has more at stake. |
| Competition for the same owner | NoBroker, Housing.com, portals, Facebook groups | the same, plus a longer history | NoBroker's 2019 brokerage TVC ran in Mumbai (https://bestmediainfo.com/2019/05/nobroker-com-launches-campaign-to-highlight-irrationality-of-paying-brokerage); the Flats and Flatmates Mumbai Facebook group was 7,000+ members in its first year (undated founder post, https://www.therodinhoods.com/post/fb-group-flats-and-flatmates-mumbais-association-with-grabhouse-a-brokerage-free-property-portal-for-rentals/), network-wide 25 lakh by 2023 (https://www.lokmattimes.com/business/how-this-facebook-based-startup-fights-illegal-discrimination-between-tenants-and-landlords-in-india). FlatMate.in lists Mumbai among its cities (competitor study, PR #48). "No brokers" is a crowded line in Mumbai; our edge is the vibe score and "numbers swap only on a yes". |
| Community support | Salu's Pune groups and college route (PRs #43, #46) | none yet | Salu's work is Pune only by Kamal's rule. Mumbai starts with paid and Instagram alone. |
| Copy | Pune names | Mumbai names | The "₹20,000 for a phone number" hook stays as written (it understates Mumbai, which is fine); area names swap per cluster: "Andheri, Powai, Thane" in P1 and P3, "Andheri or Malad flat" in P2. |
| Languages | English, Hindi, Marathi | English, Hindi, Marathi | same |

## 7 · Recommendation

**Pune first, Mumbai in month three.** Pune costs ₹77,500 before GST (about ₹91,450 with GST) for a 400-listing
target on a known click cost basis of one; it has Salu's groups and the college route behind it, and the founder is
there. Mumbai costs ₹1,08,500 before GST (about ₹1,28,030 with GST) for a 360-listing target on a click cost I can
only estimate at 1.5× Pune, with no community work and a more crowded "no brokers" message, though with the bigger
owner prize (a month's brokerage on a ₹43,000 rent). Both at once is ₹1,86,000 before GST (about ₹2,19,480 with GST),
or ₹77,500 on the two lean plans, and splits one person's attention across two learning phases in the same month,
which is how neither gets read properly. If Kamal wants Mumbai first anyway, the plan above is ready; the honest
condition is a day-7 re-forecast, because the one number I cannot source is what a Mumbai click costs us.
