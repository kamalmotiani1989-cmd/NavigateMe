## What could and could not be confirmed

- **No public source gives Indian search volumes or click costs for rental terms.** Searches for volumes of "pg in pune",
  "flatmate pune" or "room for rent in pune" returned listing pages only. The only reliable source is Google's own
  Keyword Planner inside Kamal's Ads account. Section 6 is a 20-minute check that turns every "estimate" below into a number.
- **Click costs are the open risk.** Agency benchmarks put Indian real-estate clicks at ₹40 to ₹120 in general and ₹80 to ₹250
  in metros, but those are for property sales (https://apexinfluence.in/blog/google-ads-benchmarks-india;
  https://www.nicodigital.com/performance-marketing/google-ads-cost-in-india-cpc-benchmarks/). Rental terms are said to cost
  less, without Indian figures. **The ₹8 to ₹15 manual bid in the earlier packs is probably too low to show at all; see section 5.**
- **NoBroker is the big buyer.** Paid search is about 27.55% of nobroker.in's traffic; its top terms are its own brand and
  "rent agreement" (https://www.similarweb.com/website/nobroker.in/, Jan 2026). "nobroker" alone is searched about 377K times a
  month in India (https://ahrefstop.com/websites/nobroker.in). We do not fight them on their brand or on rent agreements.
- **No housing-ad restrictions in India.** Google's housing rules for personalised ads apply in the US and Canada
  (https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9997418); Meta's special ad category for housing applies to the US, Canada and
  Europe (https://www.jonloomer.com/special-ad-categories-meta-ads/). Read Google's September 2026 policy update before launch
  (https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/17598957); its content could not be seen from here.

## 1 · How the keywords are chosen

1. **Intent over volume.** We pay for a sign-up that lists a room (Pune plan target), so the best keywords are the ones a person
   types at the moment the plan's three personas name: a flatmate leaving, a deposit on the line, an empty flat.
2. **Pune and its areas in the keyword.** City- and area-named searches are the people actually renting there, and they are
   cheaper than generic terms (my estimate). Area names are the app's own spellings.
3. **Phrase and exact match only for month one.** No broad match until the campaign has 30 sign-ups a month and moves to
   Maximise conversions; broad match on rental words pulls in sales, jobs and PG searches (my estimate, from the negatives below).
4. **Tier A starts on day 1. Tier B starts in week 2 with what Tier A leaves. Tier C we do not buy.**

## 5 · Bids and budget (changes to the Pune plan)

- **Raise the starting cap.** Start with manual CPC at **₹25 maximum** per click (my estimate between the pack's ₹15 and the
  ₹40-plus metro sales benchmarks), enhanced CPC off. On day 2 check two numbers in Google Ads: impression share and the search
  terms report. If impression share is under 30% on Tier A, raise to ₹35; if the search terms are wrong, add negatives before
  raising anything.
- **Same ₹1,000 a day.** At ₹25 a click that is about 40 clicks a day. With the plan's under-₹200 cost per listing, that needs about
  one listing for every eight clicks; the day-7 checkpoint tells us if Google can carry that or if money moves to Meta.
- **Switch to Maximise conversions** once there are 30 sign-ups in 30 days (pack rule, unchanged).
