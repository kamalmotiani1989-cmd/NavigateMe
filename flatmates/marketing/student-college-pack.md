## A · Instagram carousel · "Before you pay a deposit in Pune: the student version"

**Format.** 1080×1350, 9 slides. Teal (#0E9AA0) text on cream; one line per slide, big; small FindMeFlatMate mark
bottom right. Slide 1 is the cover. No app screens on slides 1 to 7 (it is a checklist, not an ad); slide 8 shows one
real screen; slide 9 is the close. Also exported as a single 1080×1920 image (slides 2 to 7 stacked) for WhatsApp
and Telegram, where the ambassador or an admin can post it as one picture.

**Slides**
1. **Before you pay a deposit in Pune.** 7 checks every student should do. (Save this.)
2. **Who lives there, and how.** Sleep times, food, guests, smoking. The listing never says it. Ask before you visit.
3. **The real monthly cost.** Rent is one number. Add maid, Wi-Fi, electricity split, water in summer. Ask for last
   month's bills.
4. **Owner or broker?** Ask straight. A broker takes a month's rent for an introduction. Many "owners" in the
   portals are not.
5. **Deposit, on paper.** Amount, what it covers, and the date it comes back. Pune co-living and PG complaints in
   2025 and 2026 are mostly about deposits that never returned.
6. **Notice period.** How many days, and what happens if a flatmate leaves early. Your deposit depends on the next
   person.
7. **Tenant verification is the owner's job.** Pune Police made tenant and PG reporting mandatory in 2026. An owner
   who asks for your ID for the police form is doing it right. One who asks for money before you have seen the room is
   not.
8. **Your number.** Don't post it in a group. Share it once you have said yes to meeting. (screen: **a request on the
   Requests tab with the vibe score; the number hidden until Accept**) Caption on slide: "On FindMeFlatMate, numbers
   swap only on a yes."
9. **Save it. Send it to the one who's room-hunting.** FindMeFlatMate: every room shows how the people there live,
   scored against you. No brokers, ever. Launching soon in Pune.

## B · One-screen explainer · "Campus ambassador, FindMeFlatMate Pune"

**Format.** 1080×1350 single image, also pasted as text in a DM or WhatsApp. Teal heading, cream background, five
short blocks. This is the page Kamal shows a student after an E-Cell talk; it is not posted publicly.

**Text**

**Campus ambassador · FindMeFlatMate Pune**

**What it is.** One student per campus, second year or above, who helps batchmates find rooms without brokers. You
share the deposit checklist and your referral link in the groups you are already in, and tell us what each group
allows. That's the job. About an hour a week.

**What you get.**
- The app's referral reward: every 2 friends who sign up with your link earn you 1 free contact, up to 5. (Free
  contacts are what seekers use to meet owners; 5 come free with every account, these are on top.)
- Early access before the public launch, for you and your batch.
- "Campus Ambassador, FindMeFlatMate" for your CV, and a LinkedIn recommendation from the founder after one season.

**What you never do.** No spamming, no adding people to groups, no DMs to strangers, no collecting anyone's number. One
post per group per week, in your own words, from your own account. If an admin warns you once, you stop in that group.

**How it works.** Your link carries your college, so we see what your campus needs. You get a five-minute check-in
with Kamal every week, and the checklist and posts come from us, ready to share.

**Launching soon.** The app is in private preview. Your first job is the deposit checklist and the early-access list,
not sign-ups. Reply to Kamal to say yes.
