# FindMeFlatMate · three Gen Z ads (scripts, on-screen text, AI-video prompt pack)

Three 20-second vertical ads (1080×1920, 30 fps) are attached as MP4s. Each one sells one benefit that
only FindMeFlatMate has, in the order Gen Z watches: a hook in the first 2 seconds, the pain, the real
app doing the thing, a one-line close. They work muted (every line is on screen) and have their own sound.

To make presenter versions with an AI video tool (Google Veo 3, Runway Gen-4, Kling 2, Pika), generate the
**presenter shots** below, 9:16, and cut them in at the listed times. Keep the real app screens from the
attached files; AI tools redraw interfaces badly. Paste the character block into every prompt so the
person stays the same across shots.

## Production notes

- **Music:** the attached tracks are simple synthesized beats (112 bpm) so they are safe to post. For paid
  campaigns, swap in a licensed track from the Meta or YouTube libraries, keep the beat drop on the first app
  screen (7.7 s, 8.2 s and 3.2 s respectively).
- **Voice-over:** built in, with Kokoro neural voices run locally (natural, not robotic): "Heart" (woman) for ad 1,
  "Michael" (man) for ad 2, "Bella" (woman) for ad 3. They speak neutral American English; the lines are the ones in
  the tables above, trimmed where a caption window was short, all at natural pace. For an Indian English voice,
  read the same lines in ElevenLabs (voice "Anika" or any Indian English Gen Z voice) and drop them on the same
  timings; music-only versions (`*-music-only.mp4`) are kept for exactly that, and for muted placements.
- **Variants worth testing:** swap the city names in the captions (Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Mumbai) and the
  first line of each hook. Each ad's hook is the variable with the biggest effect on cost per install.
- **Sizes:** 9:16 as delivered for Reels and Shorts. For a 1:1 feed version crop to the centre 1080×1080 of the
  phone section and move captions to the top band.
- **Landing:** point the link to findmeflatmate.com/?r=<campaign code> so sign-ups carry the campaign
  through the app's attribution (utm parameters are read on first visit).
