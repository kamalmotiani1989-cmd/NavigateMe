# FindMeFlatMate · Pune Instagram creatives (Hinglish, static)

Written by Mark, 13 November 2026, on Kamal's request via King. Reference style: a competitor's Instagram creative
(bold Hinglish caps in black and orange, a short tagline, an orange web-address button, logo top left, a photo of
young people with a phone). We took the style only: no name, logo or wording from that ad.

**Files** (repo root, not published): `design/pune-creatives/`
- `feed/<id>-feed.png`, 1080×1350, ten creatives · `story/<id>-story.png`, 1080×1920, the same ten
- `photo-guides/<id>-feed-guide.png` and `-story-guide.png`: the same layouts with the **photo area** outlined and labelled.
  Drop a photo into that rounded panel (left of the phone, behind it); the panel is a flat pastel until then.
- `contact-sheet.png`: all ten feed creatives on one page.

**How they were made.** HTML and CSS rendered with Playwright: Urbanist, text #1C1917, key words #C2410C, small text
#57534E, `logo-icon.svg` and `wordmark.svg` from `flatmates/icons`, the app's pastels for the photo panels. Every phone
screen is a real screen from the app's demo mode at 390 wide: demo data only, no real people or numbers. The vibe
card was cropped above its line counting demo homes, so no creative shows a count of homes or people.

**Rules kept.** Every claim is from `flatmates/README.md`. Call to action is "Launching soon · findmeflatmate.com".
No alcohol in any image, line or prompt. Parties say 21+ (README, Party rules, 10 Nov). Posting says "only area and
rent" (README 21d), never "two minutes", even though the app's own form says it.

**Links.** Scheme: `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=pune_creatives&utm_content=<id>`. Owner and
lister creatives (c02, c06, c10) also carry `host=Pune`, so they land on the owner page.

**AI photo prompts.** For each, paste the shared style block first:
> Photoreal, vertical 4:5, natural window light, a modest middle-class Pune flat. Indian people in their early to
> mid 20s, casual clothes. No logos, no brand names, no text, no signs, no alcohol, no cigarettes. Leave the right
> third of the frame calm and uncluttered (the phone mock-up sits there).
