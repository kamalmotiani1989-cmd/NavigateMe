Security

How we protect your data, plainly, including what isn't done yet. We hold no security certification. Last updated [TO FILL: date published]. What we collect and why is in our privacy policy.

What we do

  • Private until both say yes. A flat's address, exact pin and contact number are stored apart from the public listing, and the database hands them out only once owner and seeker have both said yes.
  • No direct access to the database. Every table has row-level security switched on, and nobody using the app can read or change a table directly. Everything goes through checked functions that look at who's asking and give back only what that person may see. Listings need you to be signed in.
  • Secret keys stay on the server. Payment, WhatsApp, SMS and notification keys are kept as server secrets, never in the app or the website. The only keys in the app are the ones meant to be public (the database's public key and a Google Maps key limited to our sites).
  • Encrypted connections. The website, the app and our services talk over HTTPS.
  • Limits against abuse. Sign-in codes: one a minute and five an hour per number, and a limit per network. Searches, opening listings, requests and posts are counted per account (or per network when signed out) and slowed down past a limit, so the listings can't be copied by a script. These counts are kept for two days.
  • 18 and over. No sign-in code is sent until you confirm you're 18 or older; the server checks it, not just the app. It is your own confirmation; we don't check ID.
  • Safe text and pages. Text people type can't carry code into someone else's screen, our pages can't be shown inside other sites, and the database's list of functions isn't published.
  • Payments. Razorpay handles card, UPI and bank details. We never see or store them.

Not done yet

  • Test sign-in. While we test, a shared test sign-in code can be used in place of a code on WhatsApp or SMS. It's stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value, and wrong tries are limited, but it is switched off before we open to everyone.
  • No record of who looked at what. We don't yet keep a log of the team's access to the database.
