Why this is not a contact database

Anyone can sell you rows. We tell you which rows matter.

Our ranking gets sharper every month because of what customers tell us afterwards. A database cannot do that.

Depth where it counts

The ₹20-crore component maker in Chakan. The regional hospital group in Indore. The distributor in Rajkot. Global databases thin out badly below the listed-company tier. That is exactly the tier most Indian suppliers sell to.

A reason on every name

Not just "Purchase Head". Which plants they run, what they commissioned last quarter, why this month is the right month. That one line is the difference between a cold call and a conversation.

The right person, not the senior one

Under ₹50 crore, the promoter decides. Between ₹50 and ₹500 crore, the functional head does and the promoter signs. Above that, procurement gatekeeps. We name the one to call first.

Where our data comes from

Every contact has a source we can name

Questions

The things people ask first

How is this different from Apollo or Lusha?

They sell you contact details once you already know who to search for. The searching is the hard part, and it is the part we do. We are also far deeper on Indian SMEs and mid-market plants, where the global databases get thin. If you already know exactly which 500 companies to call, Apollo is cheaper and you should use it.

Why is a contact ₹100 when Apollo works out to ₹17?

Because you are not buying the same thing. Apollo gives you a row. We give you a ranked industry, the right job title for that company's size, and a line on why this person is worth calling this month. If all you want is rows, we are the wrong product and we will say so.

What if a contact is wrong?

Press the button next to it and the credit goes straight back to your account. No form, no email, no argument. It also tells us to re-check that record, so it helps us as much as you.

Do you cover my industry?

We are deepest in auto components, engineering, pharma and medical supply, chemicals, food processing, textiles and building materials. Other industries work, but with less depth for now. Run the free five and judge for yourself — it costs you nothing to find out.

Is this legal?

Yes, and we would rather over-explain it than under. We use business contact information published under a legal obligation (MCA, GST, tenders) or published by the company itself (its website, a directory it joined, an exhibition it registered for). We do not buy lists and we do not scrape past logins. Every record carries a source and a date, and anyone can ask to be removed.

Can I export to my CRM?

CSV export comes with any paid pack. Direct CRM integrations are not built yet — we would rather get the targeting right first than add plumbing nobody uses.

What about calling rules and DND?

Business numbers on the public record are a different matter from consumer telemarketing, but TRAI's rules on promotional calling still apply to you as the caller. We include a short primer when you sign up, and a first-touch email template that stays on the right side of it.
